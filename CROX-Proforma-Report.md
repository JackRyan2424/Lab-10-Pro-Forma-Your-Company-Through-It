##  17. Organic growth: explain the ABG choice

 Organic growth is growth from the existing business, excluding acquisition-driven expansion; company definitions may also adjust currency or disposals. For CROX, the disclosed 2023 and 2024 DTC comparable-sales measures cover qualifying existing stores and websites, while reported growth covers the whole company; neither constant-currency growth nor DTC growth is a confirmed consolidated organic rate.

 Using the lecture's supplied 1.8% rather than 4.7% for ABG is a way to forecast the existing dealership base without repeating acquisition-driven sales growth unless acquisition spending is also modeled. ABG confirms it assesses organic performance on a same-store basis in its 2025 10-K, Item 7 ; the exact 1.8% video calculation is not independently verified here and is treated as a course assumption, not a newly confirmed filing metric.
